How Memora helps

{{ icon('camera') }}

Faces and familiarity

Recognises the people the family has added and says who they are, then plays a message in their own voice.

{{ icon('mic') }}

Voices and sweet memories

Family photos and recorded messages the patient can play any time, offered when Memora notices they're upset.

{{ icon('pill') }}

Medicines and reminders

Spoken reminders for medicines, meals and plans. "When is my next medicine?" is one tap. Missed ones alert the family.

{{ icon('package') }}

Finds everyday things

The patient's phone notices their keys, glasses or medicine box and keeps the last photo of where each one was.

{{ icon('house') }}

Guides you home

Shows how far home is and opens walking directions to the home address the family saved.

{{ icon('circle-alert') }}

SOS and safe zones

One tap sends an alert with the location to every relative. Leaving the safe zone does too.

{{ icon('shield-check') }}

Private by design

  • Faces are kept as numbers (embeddings), never as photos.
  • Camera frames without one of the patient's things are thrown away straight away.
  • Voice messages, memories and photos are encrypted on the server.
  • No cloud face service is used for anyone unless the family turns it on for that person.
  • Memora is a website: it uses the camera and location only while it is open on the phone.
